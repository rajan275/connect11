Fearless Warrior • Founder of Karora Singha Misl

Biography of a Fearless Warrior

Baba Sham Singh Narla, a significant figure in Punjab's history, was a fearless warrior and the founder of the Karora Singha Misl. He was a Jat of Narli village and played a crucial role in the early origins of the Sikh Confederacy.

Sham Singh was known for his bravery, devotion, and leadership. He fought against the Mughal forces and the invading armies of Nadir Shah, standing as a symbol of resistance and courage.

His grandson, Rattan Singh Bhangu, continued his legacy as a warrior, ensuring that the spirit of defiance and protection of Punjab lived on.
